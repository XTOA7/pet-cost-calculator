Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how the International Pet Cost Calculator ("this site", "we", "us") handles information when you visit.

Information you enter

The calculator asks for details about the pet and your preferences. This information is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to us or stored on any server. We do not collect, log, or share it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies for basic functionality and, if advertising is enabled, for personalised advertising. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Third-party advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third parties, including Amazon Associates and potentially Google AdSense. Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (if AdSense is added).
  • Amazon may use cookies to track affiliate sales. You can review Amazon's privacy policy on their website.

Links to other sites

This site links to external websites (including Amazon). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., UK/EU under GDPR, or Australia/NZ under local privacy laws), you may have specific rights regarding your data. Since we do not collect personal data through this site, these rights primarily apply to any third-party services you interact with. For questions, contact us at quicklifecalc@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
