About This Calculator

The UK Pet Cost Calculator is a free, independent tool that helps prospective pet owners understand the true financial commitment of owning a dog or cat.

Why this exists

Many people underestimate the cost of pet ownership. Charities like the PDSA and Dogs Trust frequently report that financial strain is a leading reason pets are surrendered. Before bringing a pet into your home, it is vital to know the first-year setup costs and the ongoing monthly expenses.

This calculator distils UK average costs for food, insurance, and routine care into a simple, realistic estimate.

Who built it

This is an independent project built by a developer with an interest in personal finance and pet welfare. It is not affiliated with any specific pet charity, insurance company, or retailer.

What it is — and isn't

This tool is for educational and planning purposes only. It does not constitute financial advice. Actual costs vary significantly based on where you live in the UK, the specific breed of the animal, and unforeseen veterinary emergencies. We accept no liability for financial decisions made based on these estimates.

Sources

The estimates are based on published UK averages from pet charities and consumer research, including data from PDSA and the RSPCA.
