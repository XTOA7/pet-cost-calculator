About This Calculator

The International Pet Cost Calculator is a free, independent tool that helps prospective pet owners understand the true financial commitment of owning a dog or cat in the UK, US, Australia, or New Zealand.

Why this exists

Many people underestimate the cost of pet ownership. Financial strain is a leading reason pets are surrendered to shelters worldwide. Before bringing a pet into your home, it is vital to know the first-year setup costs, the ongoing monthly expenses, and the potential lifetime cost.

This calculator distils average costs for food, insurance, routine care, and grooming into a simple, realistic estimate. It adjusts automatically for your country's currency and local pricing averages.

Who built it

This is an independent project built by a developer with an interest in personal finance and pet welfare. It is not affiliated with any specific pet charity, insurance company, or retailer.

What it is — and isn't

This tool is for educational and planning purposes only. It does not constitute financial advice. Actual costs vary significantly based on where you live, the specific breed of the animal, and unforeseen veterinary emergencies. We accept no liability for financial decisions made based on these estimates.
